# 🔊 Voxx

**Open sound. No limits.**

Voxx is an open-source, modular speaker system designed to deliver **serious sound quality** at **any size** — from a pocket-sized core speaker to a fully connected, room-shaking stack.

Build one. Build many.  
Connect them all.

---

## 🎧 What is Voxx?

Voxx is a **DIY-friendly, open-source audio project** focused on three core ideas:

- **Excellent sound quality** — even from the smallest speaker
- **Scalability** — connect multiple speakers seamlessly
- **Freedom** — open hardware, open software, open community

Whether you want a **compact portable speaker**, a **living-room system**, or a **modular party setup**, Voxx grows with you.

---

## 📦 Modular by Design

Voxx is built around different speaker sizes that all work together:

- **Voxx Dott**  
  Ultra-compact core speaker. Small, portable, but surprisingly powerful.

All Voxx speakers are designed to **connect, sync, and scale**.

---

## 🔗 Connect Everything

Voxx is designed to support:
- Multi-speaker setups
- Wireless or wired linking (depending on build)
- Future mesh / sync concepts
- Stereo, mono, and grouped playback

One speaker is good.  
Many speakers are better.
